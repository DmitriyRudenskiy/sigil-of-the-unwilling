# Adjacent systems dependency map (task 6.1)

This is an audit of the currently existing code paths, not a declaration that legacy rules already participate in the campaign catalog. Primary runtime discovery: `WorldBootstrap` registers `CityTurnProcessor` (priority 5), `EconomicTurnProcessor` (10), `CityIncomeProcessor` (15), `DemographicTurnProcessor` (20), and threat processors; `WorldEventRouter._run_turn_scheduler()` supplies the existing `City` objects to one deterministic `TurnScheduler`.

| Adjacent mechanic | Existing owner / current data | Dependency for unified campaign-city behavior | Duplicate or conflict to prevent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction timing | `CityBuildingService.build_building()` immediately spends legacy industry/followers and creates `UniqueBuilding`; catalog contract has `construction_turns`, and `CampaignBuildingPlacement` validates 52-cell footprint/terrain/occupancy. No campaign construction queue is wired. | A campaign build action must reserve registered costs atomically, validate the 52-cell site, and resolve the declared turn counter before activating the instance. | Do not also spend legacy `industry`/followers or start an independent city-level timer. |
| Storage and logistics | `ResourceContext`/`ResourceRegistry` provide atomic flows; `CityData.food_stockpile` is now a compatibility facade over its food balance. Other legacy `storage` entries remain separate. `LogisticsCalculator` applies distance/road multipliers to legacy building production. | Campaign MVP stock is limited to registered `food`, `wood`, and `iron` transactions; campaign building capacities and logistics effects remain undeclared/unwired. | Do not mirror food across `food_stockpile` and `ResourceContext`, or apply logistics twice. Do not introduce a second campaign dictionary for stock. |
| Trade | `MarketSystem` sells legacy goods at fixed rates and adds proceeds to `storage.industry` (with prosperity/specialization multipliers). No campaign ledger adapter exists. | Either explicitly adapt trade into registered goods via catalog recipes/actions or leave it outside MVP; transaction must be ledger-backed. | `gold`/`industry`, source-game currencies, and market conversion rates are not campaign stock or unlock gates. |
| Upkeep and repair | `EconomicTurnProcessor` processes legacy `UniqueBuilding` upkeep and catalog-shaped campaign instance upkeep; inactive/ruined campaign states suppress production. No campaign repair action/queue is connected. | Upkeep remains in the same turn ledger; repair must be an explicit cost/state transition, preserving the instance and capacity. | No second upkeep pass, implicit repair, or entity deletion when a provider/capacity becomes inactive. |
| Population growth/migration | `CityGrowthService` consumes the shared food stock and creates legacy followers; `MigrationProcessor` uses reputation/hero charisma; `DemographicTurnProcessor` attaches characters and resolves their needs. Campaign `PopUnit` stores ancestry/archetype and work assignment; `campaign_group_state` persists group satisfaction/unmet turns. | Need/staffing/group state now resolve in `EconomicTurnProcessor` and serialize through `CitySerializer`; Mvp campaign-save party/population remains a separate save schema. | Do not count legacy followers and campaign residents twice, apply character needs as group needs, or infer archetype from a legacy city level. Unknown legacy ancestry stays empty, not guessed. |
| Seasonal/environment effects | `WorldSeasons.production_mult()` modifies legacy production and advances after each world turn; placement contract has terrain tags, but no campaign seasonal/environment evaluator. | If campaign seasonality is in scope, pass the existing season state once into the campaign economy and declare affected roles/effects in data. | Do not multiply both `WorldSeasons` and catalog state effects; source-game seasons/weather are not automatically imported. |
| Threat/defense ordering | `CityTurnProcessor` invokes raid processor; `CityService.defense_strength()` now includes active campaign static defense; `RaidSystem` reports the deterministic contribution. | Keep raid resolution after campaign-building state/upkeep changes in the established turn order, and persist defense state in the same city save. | Do not add a separate pressure/raid clock or calculate the same defense contribution twice. |

## Safe dependency order

1. The source-backed runtime catalog is now populated; verify new rows against the observed evidence gate before admission. Construction actions and UI remain the highest-impact uncovered follow-on mechanics.
2. Route production, needs, group modifiers, upkeep, defense, training costs, and turn ledger through the existing city/runtime contracts; serialize city group state and preserve MVP party-save ownership (tasks 5.1–5.3).
3. Only then expand trade, repair, logistics, or seasonal modifiers; first reuse their existing owner or explicitly exclude them.

The remaining highest-impact adjacent work is construction actions and UI, not a second scheduler or city adapter. The catalog already rejects candidate/unknown evidence at admission; keep those systems deferred rather than adding parallel city state.
